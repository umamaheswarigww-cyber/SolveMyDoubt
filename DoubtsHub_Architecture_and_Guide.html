Academic Collaboration Engine

DoubtsHub (SolveMyDoubt)

A peer-to-peer and faculty-verified academic doubt resolution ecosystem designed with zero-doubt escalation guarantees, realtime synchronization, and dual-responsive PC & mobile architectures.

1. Platform Overview & Core Mission

DoubtsHub (formerly SolveMyDoubt) is an educational peer network built to bridge the gap between student confusion and timely, faculty-validated academic mastery.

🎯 Frictionless Inquiry

Students post doubts with free-form explanations, handwritten diagrams, code snippets, or formulas without complex configuration barriers.

⏳ 12-Hour Escalation Radar

Unanswered doubts automatically escalate through urgency tiers (+15 reputation bounties) before alerting department faculty triage desks.

🏛️ Faculty Verification & Governance

Professors officially stamp solutions with the "Faculty Verified" badge and have moderation rights to immediately purge improper or abusive posts.

📱 Dual Responsive Design

Custom mobile bottom navigation and Instagram-style threaded bottom sheets on smartphones; full dual-sidebar command view on desktop PCs.

4. How It Works (Core Workflows)

A. The Lifecycle of an Academic Doubt

  1. Drafting & Asking: A student opens the simplified Ask a Doubt page. They type a title, describe the issue freely, and optionally attach a photo or handwritten formula diagram. Autosave preserves their text locally in case of page refresh.
  2. Live Publication: Once submitted, the question is stored in the PostgreSQL database and immediately broadcast over Supabase Realtime channels. Other active users see a live notification toast without refreshing.
  3. Community Discussion: Learners click on any doubt card to open the Instagram-style threaded comment window. Anyone can post step-by-step explanations, code snippets, or annotated solution images.
  4. Voting & Best Solution: Peers upvote helpful explanations. The original asker can mark a solution as Accepted, triggering a congratulatory confetti animation and awarding reputation points to the contributor.

B. Zero Doubts Left Behind (Rescue Escalation Radar)

Unlike traditional forums where hard questions get ignored, DoubtsHub features an automatic escalation radar:

  • 0 to 4 Hours: Question sits in standard student peer recommended feed.
  • 4 to 12 Hours: If unanswered, the question is flagged with the Fast Clarification badge and routed to the Rescue Queue with a +15 Reputation Bounty to incentivize peer solvers.
  • 12+ Hours: The question escalates directly into the Faculty Review Queue for professor triage.

C. Faculty Desk, Verification & Moderation

  • Role Lock on Sign-In: Upon authenticating, users choose whether to enter as a Student Learner or Faculty Educator. The role remains strictly locked during their active session to prevent fraudulent role tampering.
  • Official Answer Verification: Faculty members have a dedicated review panel where they can attach a verified seal (ShieldCheck badge) and official feedback notes to mathematically sound answers.
  • Abuse Purge & Question Deletion: Faculty members possess unilateral moderation authority. Clicking the trash icon on any doubt opens a confirmation dialog that deletes improper or abusive doubts and their answers immediately.

D. Client-Side Image Annotation & Download Pipeline

Every posted image or diagram includes an interactive overlay:

  • Download: Allows any learner or professor to save diagrams directly with one click.
  • Annotate: Launches a canvas modal equipped with a freehand pen, arrow pointers, highlight rectangles, text input, custom color palette, and undo history. Users can annotate diagrams directly in the browser and re-attach them to explanations.

6. Mobile-First vs. PC Desktop Architecture

🖥️ PC Desktop Experience
  • Persistent Left Sidebar: Instant navigation to Workspace, Rescue Queue, Library, and Leaderboard.
  • Right Sidebar Widget: Realtime rescue metrics, active course filters, and community leaderboard.
  • Wide Header: Full search bar with Ctrl+K shortcut listener and role badge.
📱 Mobile Smartphone Experience
  • Sticky Bottom Navigation: Elevated "+ Ask" center action, Home, Explore, Rescue, and Saved shortcuts.
  • 100dvh Dynamic Viewport: Full-screen modal sheets that never get blocked by mobile browser navigation bars.
  • Clean Header: Role badges tucked neatly inside dropdown to maximize brand and search bar touch space.
  • Horizontal Scroll Tabs: Course and feed filter pills swipe smoothly with zero overflow clipping.
